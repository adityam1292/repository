# Design drafts

Six designs for the same artist website. Open `index.html` in this folder to see them all, or open
each draft's own `index.html`.

| Draft | Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `1-beige-timeline` | Beige timeline with all 278 works, critics' quotes and Studio 9 (own copy of its data) |
| 2 | `2-minimal-white` | White professional format with a slideshow of 65 selected works (own copy of its data) |
| 3 | `../index.html` | The main site: professional structure on beige, with year chapters |
| 4–6 | below | Modelled on working artists' websites |

Drafts 4, 5 and 6 read the same `../data/site.js` and `../data/works.js` as the main site, so any text or
artwork change appears in all of them. Drafts 1 and 2 keep a snapshot of the data as it was when they were made.

| Draft | Modelled on | What's different |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `4-archive` | bhartikher.com | Exhibitions archive as the front page; works as a grid of series, each with its own page |
| `5-studio` | jitishkallat.com | Fixed monospace header, rotating banner, works grid with medium and year filters, a page per artwork |
| `6-gallery` | suneelmamadapur.com | Full-screen entrance photo, sage background, Exhibitions drop-down with a page per show, works in scrolling strips |
